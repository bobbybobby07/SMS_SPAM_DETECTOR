SMS / CHECK Open detector

A machine-learning project

Spot suspicious texts before you reply.

This demo uses a text-classification model to estimate whether an SMS looks like spam. Paste a message into the detector to see its prediction and confidence.

01 / Method

From words to a prediction

  1. 01

    The message is converted into word and two-word phrase counts.

  2. 02

    A Multinomial Naive Bayes classifier compares those patterns with examples from the training data.

  3. 03

    The page displays the predicted label and the model's confidence for that label.

02 / Model notes

What the demo uses

Training data
SMS Spam Collection, 5,574 messages
Text features
Bag-of-words unigrams and bigrams
Classifier
Multinomial Naive Bayes with smoothing
Message limit
1,000 characters per prediction

A useful signal, not a guarantee

Check before you act

Automated predictions can be wrong, especially for unusual wording or messages unlike the training examples. Never use a prediction alone to decide whether a link, caller, or request for money is trustworthy.

Messages are classified for the request and are not stored by this app.
